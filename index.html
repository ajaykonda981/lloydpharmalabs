About the company

Welcome to Lloyd Pharmalabs

Lloyd Pharmalabs is a privately owned company established under the Companies Act in Hyderabad, India. Our state-of-the-art R&D facility is located at Pashamylaram Industrial Park, Hyderabad, Telangana, and our manufacturing facility is located at APIIC Industrial Park, Attivaram, Andhra Pradesh.

Infrastructure

Built for high-pressure, high-precision chemistry

The facility is spread over a built-up area of 65,000 sq. ft. and includes three state-of-the-art production blocks covering 2,800 sq. meters, with a total capacity of over 150 KL. Designed in accordance with cGMP standards — including QC, warehouse and production areas — and engineered to safely handle high-pressure reactions up to 20 kg/cm².
